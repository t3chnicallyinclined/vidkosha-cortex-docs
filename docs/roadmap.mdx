A simple Now/Next/Later view for operators and builders. This is deliberately written in terms of **operator services** and **what becomes monetizable**.

This is not a promise of exact dates. It’s a sequencing plan that prioritizes: (1) real usage, (2) measurable operator work, (3) settlement/verifiability upgrades.

## Now

- **WAL + projection loop robustness:** replayable streams, idempotent projections, visible lag, and “rebuild from log” discipline.
- **Archive market is real:** pack manifests and integrity checks, clear hot vs cold retrieval, paid archival retrieval metering.
- **Operator work is legible:** usage events → aggregation → payout batches.
- **Operator service templates:** starter services that are easy to deploy and sell (retrieval, pack serving, tools/MCP workflows; optional embeddings/inference).
- **Observability-first:** service latency + error budgets + consumer lag dashboards that connect directly to routing and payouts.
- **Chain integration is gated during buildout:** ship operator revenue first, then flip on CKB dev testnet and harden anchoring/settlement.

Success looks like:

- operators can deploy a service and get paid for real requests
- users can choose between SLO/price tiers
- the system can be audited from logs/manifests when something goes wrong

## Next

- **Better query planning:** hot-first retrieval, priced cold expansion, integrity verification, predictable “what will this cost?” behavior.
- **Stronger routing:** SLO tiers, geo/egress-aware selection, fairness policies, and explicit degradation paths.
- **More service products:** pack-backed vertical offerings (media packs, compliance packs, market monitoring, incident intelligence).
- **Catalog-quality discovery:** marketplace discovery/search backed by an index (Helix), not subject naming conventions.
- **Auditability upgrades:** clearer dispute windows, batch finalization rules, and optional hash-only checkpointing.

Success looks like:

- operators can specialize by service and differentiate on SLO/coverage
- the marketplace feels like a searchable catalog with clear pricing
- payouts are predictable and explainable (“why did I earn X?”)

## Later

Later (only when justified by scale/adversarial pressure):

- optional on-chain commitments for payout batch roots and/or pack manifests
- optional on-chain settlement modes
- stronger enforcement mechanisms (staking/escrow/slashing) for premium SLO tiers
