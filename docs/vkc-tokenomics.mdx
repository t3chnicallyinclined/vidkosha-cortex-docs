This is the first rendition of the simplified Vidkosha Cortex DAO economics and `VKC` token model.
It is intentionally designed to be **operator-friendly**, **investor-legible**, and **low-complexity**.

**Key simplifications (v1):**
- **First-party operators only** (no open marketplace, no bidding, no third-party discovery).
- **Stable-first accounting and payouts**: customers pay in fiat or stablecoins; operators are paid in **USDC**.
- `VKC` is **fixed-supply** and used for **governance + staking**, not as the default payment currency.
- **POL is optional** and only turns on once there is real revenue + a safe primary liquidity venue.

> Older/internal docs may reference `NCC` or `NCRX`. The canonical token name going forward is **`VKC`**.
> The legacy architecture blueprint remains in `contexts/dao_financial_architecture.md` (unchanged).

## Goals
- Make operator work **measurable and payable** via `usage_event → payout_batch`.
- Keep end-user UX **SaaS simple** (USD pricing; fiat checkout; optional crypto checkout).
- Build a credible **treasury + reserve policy** funded by real revenue.
- Enable **Protocol-Owned Liquidity (POL)** later without turning tokenomics into the product.

## Non-goals (v1)
- No per-request escrow, disputes, evaluator courts, or open specialist marketplace.
- No inflationary emissions schedule.
- No requirement for end users to hold `VKC`.

## Roles
- **Customers**: pay for a ChatGPT-like app + agentic workflows, in fiat or stablecoins.
- **Treasury (LLC/DAO-controlled)**: receives revenue, pays operator costs, maintains reserves, optionally deploys POL.
- **Operators (first-party, v1)**: run inference/RAG/workflow services; emit usage events; receive USDC payouts.
- **`VKC` holders**: govern slow-changing protocol parameters; stake to be eligible for operator privileges (v2+).

## Canonical money flow (stable-first)
**Unit of account:** USD (or USDC-equivalent).

1. Customer pays **$X** (fiat) or **X USDC** (crypto checkout).
2. Treasury books revenue in USD/USDC.
3. Runtime emits `usage_event` records for measurable work units.
4. At epoch close (weekly recommended), `usage_event`s aggregate into a `payout_batch`.
5. Treasury pays first-party operators in **USDC** from the payout batch.

Why this is the default:
- Operators have real-world costs (GPUs/cloud) denominated in fiat/stables.
- Stable payouts reduce forced token selling and keep the network reliable.

## `VKC` design (fixed-supply governance + staking)
### Purpose
- **Governance**: vote on *slow-changing* parameters that matter for operators and treasury stewardship.
- **Staking** (v2+): “skin in the game” for operator eligibility and higher-trust roles.

### What `VKC` is *not* (v1)
- Not the default customer payment token.
- Not an emissions-driven rewards token.

### Supply
- **Fixed total supply** (exact number TBD).
- No protocol minting after genesis.

### Typical/simple allocation buckets (ranges; finalize later)
- **Team + builders** (vested): 15–30%
- **Treasury / ecosystem** (locked + governed): 25–50%
- **Investors** (vested): 10–25%
- **Operator/community programs** (vested/locked): 10–25%

**Vesting best practices (typical):**
- Team: 4y vest with 1y cliff.
- Investors: 1–3y vest (depending on round), with lockups.
- Treasury: timelocked + governed spend policy.

## Governance scope (narrow by design)
`VKC` governance should target parameters that operators can plan around and that can be audited:
- **Model registry:** which base models/versions are allowed in production routing.
- **Service definitions:** what counts as a billable unit per service.
- **Treasury policy parameters:** reserve floor, payout epoch length, POL deploy caps.
- **Operator eligibility rules (v2+):** stake thresholds, minimum hardware/SLO class.
- **Security upgrades:** upgrades that change receipt/anchoring behavior (if enabled).

**Not governed day-to-day:** incident response, abuse mitigation, hotfix rollouts.

## Treasury policy (simple guardrails)
In v1, implement a minimal `treasury_policy` (off-chain config + published snapshots):
- **Reserve floor:** maintain N months runway in USDC before optional POL/buyback actions.
- **Payout pool:** weekly budget derived from revenue and runway constraints.
- **POL budget cap:** explicit ceiling (starts at 0; turns on only after stability).

## POL (Protocol-Owned Liquidity)
POL protects against “liquidity disappears overnight” dynamics by having the treasury **own LP positions**.

**V1 posture:** POL is **off** (or extremely small) until:
- consistent revenue exists,
- a primary venue exists with real stable liquidity,
- and treasury operations are secured (multisig, spend limits, reporting).

## Optional value-capture (deferred)
Once revenue is consistent and operations are mature, governance may enable one of:
- **Buy and lock**: buy `VKC` and lock it (reduces float).
- **Buy and distribute to stakers**: complex; do carefully.
- **Buy and burn**: simplest mechanically; irreversible.

In v1, default is **reserves + payouts + (optional) POL**, not buybacks.

## Roadmap (economics)
- **v1:** first-party operators + USDC payouts + published treasury policy snapshots; no marketplace; `VKC` spec finalized.
- **v1.5:** hash-only checkpointing of `payout_batch` roots and policy snapshots (optional chain lane).
- **v2:** introduce `VKC` staking gates for operator privileges; delegation optional; keep payouts stable-first.
- **v3:** open operator set only when adversarial pressure justifies it; then consider slashing/dispute complexity.
